Linen

An open book on a linen-woven paper tile. Its rust ribbon marks your exact place, the way Linen reopens a book where you left it. Everything is drawn with Canvas 2D in icon.js on Apple's 1024 px icon grid, in the reader's own theme colours.

  • Tile: Paper #F7F3EC fading toward Sepia #F1E6D2, with a faint woven thread for the name. The weave is omitted below 128 px.
  • Book: two curved page stacks on a rust cover (#8C4A2F). The pages are blank: this is a quiet reader.
  • Ribbon: the one saturated mark, readable down to 16 px.
